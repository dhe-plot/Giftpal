# 📧 GIFTPAL Investor Email Outreach Templates

## 🎯 Email Strategy Overview

### Outreach Sequence
1. **Initial Introduction Email** - First contact with compelling hook
2. **Follow-up Email** - If no response after 1 week
3. **Value-Add Email** - Share relevant market insights (after 2 weeks)
4. **Final Touch Email** - Last attempt with urgency (after 1 month)

### Email Timing
- **Best Days**: Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday
- **Best Times**: 9-11 AM, 2-4 PM (Nigerian time)
- **Follow-up Interval**: 7 days between emails
- **Maximum Sequence**: 4 emails over 6 weeks

## 📊 Email Performance Tracking

### Metrics to Track
- **Open Rate**: Target 25-30%
- **Response Rate**: Target 5-10%
- **Meeting Conversion**: Target 20-30% of responses
- **Investment Interest**: Target 10-15% of meetings

### A/B Testing Elements
- **Subject Lines**: Test urgency vs. curiosity vs. direct
- **Email Length**: Test short vs. detailed versions
- **Call-to-Action**: Test specific vs. open-ended asks
- **Personalization**: Test high vs. low personalization

### Follow-up Schedule
- **Day 0**: Initial email
- **Day 7**: Follow-up email
- **Day 14**: Value-add email (market insights/updates)
- **Day 28**: Final touch email
- **Day 35**: Move to "nurture" sequence

## 🎯 Email Best Practices

### Subject Line Guidelines
- Keep under 50 characters
- Include specific details (funding amount, company name)
- Avoid spam trigger words
- Test different approaches (question vs. statement)

### Email Structure
1. **Hook**: Compelling opening line
2. **Context**: Brief company description
3. **Traction**: Key metrics and achievements
4. **Ask**: Clear, specific request
5. **Close**: Professional sign-off with contact info

### Personalization Tips
- Reference specific portfolio companies
- Mention mutual connections
- Highlight relevant investor focus areas
- Include recent company/investor news
- Show knowledge of investor's background

This comprehensive email strategy provides professional, compelling outreach templates for every type of investor while maintaining authenticity and building genuine relationships.

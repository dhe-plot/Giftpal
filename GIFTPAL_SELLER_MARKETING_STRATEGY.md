# 🎁 GIFTPAL Pre-Launch Seller Marketing Strategy

## Executive Summary

This comprehensive marketing strategy is designed to attract and onboard 1,000+ quality sellers across Lagos and Abuja before GIFTPAL's official launch. Our integrated approach combines above-the-line (ATL) and below-the-line (BTL) marketing to create maximum market penetration and seller excitement.

## 🎯 Target Seller Segments

### Primary Targets (70% of acquisition focus)

#### 1. Established Gift Shop Owners
- **Profile**: Physical stores with 2-10 years experience
- **Location**: Victoria Island, Ikeja, Lekki (Lagos); Wuse, Garki, Maitama (Abuja)
- **Pain Points**: Limited online presence, seasonal sales fluctuations
- **Value Proposition**: Expand reach, increase sales, professional online presence

#### 2. Artisan Creators & Craftspeople
- **Profile**: Handmade goods creators, local artisans
- **Location**: Arts & crafts markets, creative hubs
- **Pain Points**: Difficulty reaching customers, pricing challenges
- **Value Proposition**: Direct access to gift buyers, fair pricing support

#### 3. Boutique Retailers
- **Profile**: Fashion accessories, jewelry, home décor specialists
- **Location**: High-end shopping areas, malls
- **Pain Points**: High rent costs, limited customer base
- **Value Proposition**: Additional revenue stream, lower overhead

### Secondary Targets (30% of acquisition focus)

#### 4. Corporate Gift Suppliers
- **Profile**: B2B gift providers, promotional item vendors
- **Location**: Business districts
- **Pain Points**: Limited B2C reach, seasonal demand
- **Value Proposition**: Year-round B2C sales, diversified revenue

#### 5. Online-First Sellers
- **Profile**: Instagram/Facebook sellers, e-commerce entrepreneurs
- **Location**: Digital-native, location-agnostic
- **Pain Points**: Platform dependency, payment processing
- **Value Proposition**: Professional platform, integrated payments

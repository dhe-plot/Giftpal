## 🎯 Personalization Guidelines

### Research Requirements (5 minutes per investor)
- [ ] Recent portfolio investments
- [ ] Investment thesis and focus areas
- [ ] Recent news or announcements
- [ ] Mutual connections
- [ ] Social media activity

### Personalization Elements
- **Opening Line**: Reference specific portfolio company or recent news
- **Value Proposition**: Align with investor's focus areas
- **Mutual Connections**: Mention shared network when relevant
- **Specific Ask**: Tailor to investor type and check size

### Email Customization Checklist
- [ ] Investor name and firm correctly spelled
- [ ] Specific portfolio company mentioned
- [ ] Investment range appropriate for investor tier
- [ ] Email template matches investor type
- [ ] Call-to-action is specific and clear
- [ ] Contact information is complete

- [ ] Prepare investor-specific pitch deck

1. **Market Size**: How did you calculate ₦2.5T market?
2. **Competition**: What about Jumia and other marketplaces?
3. **Unit Economics**: Walk through LTV/CAC calculations
4. **AI Technology**: How does your recommendation engine work?
5. **Seller Acquisition**: How do you plan to onboard 1000+ sellers?
6. **Monetization**: When will you achieve profitability?
7. **Team**: Why is this team uniquely positioned to win?
8. **Use of Funds**: Detailed breakdown of ₦2.5B usage

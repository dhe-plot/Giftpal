### Creative Asset Requirements

#### Image Ads
- **Dimensions**: 1080x1080px (Square), 1200x628px (Landscape)
- **Text Overlay**: Maximum 20% of image
- **Brand Elements**: Logo, colors, consistent styling
- **Call-to-Action**: Clear, action-oriented buttons

#### Video Ads
- **Length**: 15-30 seconds optimal
- **Aspect Ratio**: 1:1 (Square), 9:16 (Stories), 16:9 (Landscape)
- **Captions**: Always include for accessibility
- **Hook**: Compelling first 3 seconds

#### Carousel Ads
- **Cards**: 3-10 cards per carousel
- **Consistency**: Uniform styling across cards
- **Progression**: Logical flow and storytelling
- **CTA**: Consistent call-to-action

## 📊 Performance Tracking & Optimization

### Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

#### Customer Acquisition Campaigns
- **Cost Per Acquisition (CPA)**: Target ₦3,000-5,000
- **Return on Ad Spend (ROAS)**: Target 4:1 minimum
- **Click-Through Rate (CTR)**: Target 2%+ for Facebook, 3%+ for Google
- **Conversion Rate**: Target 3-5% from ad clicks
- **Customer Lifetime Value (CLV)**: Track 6-month and 12-month values

#### Seller Acquisition Campaigns
- **Cost Per Lead (CPL)**: Target ₦1,500-3,000
- **Lead Quality Score**: Based on application completion and verification
- **Seller Activation Rate**: Percentage who complete onboarding
- **Seller Success Rate**: Percentage who make first sale within 30 days

#### Brand Awareness Campaigns
- **Cost Per Thousand Impressions (CPM)**: Benchmark and optimize
- **Reach and Frequency**: Optimal frequency 3-5 times per week
- **Brand Recall**: Survey-based measurement
- **Social Media Mentions**: Track organic brand mentions

### Optimization Strategy

#### Weekly Optimization Tasks
- Review campaign performance and adjust budgets
- Pause underperforming ad sets and creatives
- Test new audiences and targeting options
- Update ad copy and creative assets
- Analyze competitor advertising activity

#### Monthly Optimization Tasks
- Comprehensive performance analysis
- Audience insights and behavior analysis
- Creative performance review and refresh
- Budget reallocation based on performance
- A/B testing of landing pages and funnels

#### Quarterly Optimization Tasks
- Complete campaign strategy review
- Audience segmentation refinement
- Creative strategy overhaul
- Platform mix optimization
- Attribution model analysis

### Testing Framework

#### Creative Testing
- **Ad Copy**: Headlines, descriptions, CTAs
- **Visuals**: Images, videos, graphics
- **Formats**: Single image, carousel, video, collection
- **Messaging**: Value propositions, offers, urgency

#### Audience Testing
- **Demographics**: Age, gender, income, education
- **Interests**: Broad vs. specific interests
- **Behaviors**: Shopping behaviors, device usage
- **Custom Audiences**: Website visitors, email lists, lookalikes

#### Placement Testing
- **Platform Comparison**: Facebook vs. Instagram vs. Google
- **Placement Comparison**: Feed vs. Stories vs. Search
- **Device Comparison**: Mobile vs. desktop performance
- **Time Testing**: Day of week and hour optimization

# 🌟 GIFTPAL Influencer Outreach Templates

## 🎯 Influencer Categories & Targeting Strategy

### Tier 1: Mega Influencers (1M+ followers)
**Target Profiles**: Celebrities, major lifestyle influencers, TV personalities
**Budget Range**: ₦500K - ₦2M per campaign
**Campaign Type**: Brand ambassadorship, major product launches
**Examples**: Toke Makinwa, Mercy Eke, Tacha, Bobrisky

### Tier 2: Macro Influencers (100K - 1M followers)
**Target Profiles**: Fashion, lifestyle, business, parenting influencers
**Budget Range**: ₦100K - ₦500K per campaign
**Campaign Type**: Product collaborations, event partnerships
**Examples**: Dimma Umeh, Fisayo Longe, Temie Giwa-Tubosun

### Tier 3: Micro Influencers (10K - 100K followers)
**Target Profiles**: Niche experts, local celebrities, emerging creators
**Budget Range**: ₦25K - ₦100K per campaign
**Campaign Type**: Product reviews, authentic recommendations
**Focus**: High engagement rates, authentic connections

### Tier 4: Nano Influencers (1K - 10K followers)
**Target Profiles**: Local community leaders, specialized creators
**Budget Range**: ₦5K - ₦25K per campaign
**Campaign Type**: Product gifting, user-generated content
**Focus**: Authentic, grassroots marketing

## 📊 Performance Metrics & KPIs

### Influencer Campaign Success Metrics
- **Reach**: Total unique accounts reached
- **Impressions**: Total number of times content was viewed
- **Engagement Rate**: (Likes + Comments + Shares) / Followers
- **Click-Through Rate**: Clicks to GIFTPAL website / Impressions
- **Conversion Rate**: Purchases / Clicks
- **Cost Per Acquisition**: Campaign cost / New customers acquired
- **Brand Mention Sentiment**: Positive/Negative/Neutral mentions

### ROI Calculation Template
```
Campaign Investment: ₦[Amount]
+ Influencer fee
+ Product costs
+ Management time
+ Platform fees

Campaign Returns: ₦[Amount]
+ Direct sales from campaign
+ New customer lifetime value
+ Brand awareness value
+ User-generated content value

ROI = (Returns - Investment) / Investment × 100
```

## 🤝 Partnership Agreement Templates

### Standard Influencer Agreement Points
1. **Deliverables**: Specific content requirements
2. **Timeline**: Content creation and posting schedule
3. **Compensation**: Payment terms and amounts
4. **Usage Rights**: How GIFTPAL can use the content
5. **Exclusivity**: Competitor restrictions during campaign
6. **Disclosure**: FTC compliance and transparency requirements
7. **Performance**: Minimum engagement or reach expectations
8. **Termination**: Conditions for ending partnership

### Content Guidelines for Influencers
- **Authenticity**: Genuine opinions and experiences required
- **Quality**: High-resolution images and clear audio
- **Brand Alignment**: Content should reflect GIFTPAL values
- **Disclosure**: Clear #ad or #sponsored hashtags
- **Approval**: Content review process before posting
- **Timing**: Optimal posting times for maximum engagement

This comprehensive influencer outreach strategy provides templates for every level of partnership while maintaining authenticity and supporting GIFTPAL's mission of connecting Nigerian gift-givers with local sellers.

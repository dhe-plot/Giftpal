# 🎨 GIFTPAL Visual Content Guidelines

## 🎯 Brand Visual Identity

### Primary Brand Colors
- **GIFTPAL Purple**: #6B46C1 (Primary brand color)
- **GIFTPAL Gold**: #F59E0B (Celebration and premium)
- **GIFTPAL Green**: #10B981 (Success and growth)
- **Neutral Gray**: #6B7280 (Supporting text)
- **Pure White**: #FFFFFF (Clean backgrounds)
- **Deep Black**: #1F2937 (High contrast text)

### Secondary Color Palette
- **Light Purple**: #A78BFA (Backgrounds and accents)
- **Warm Gold**: #FCD34D (Highlights and CTAs)
- **Soft Green**: #6EE7B7 (Success states)
- **Light Gray**: #F3F4F6 (Subtle backgrounds)
- **Cream**: #FFFBEB (Warm backgrounds)

### Typography Hierarchy

#### Primary Font: Inter
- **Headlines**: Inter Bold (32px+)
- **Subheadings**: Inter Semibold (24px)
- **Body Text**: Inter Regular (16px)
- **Captions**: Inter Medium (14px)
- **Small Text**: Inter Regular (12px)

#### Secondary Font: Playfair Display
- **Brand Headlines**: Playfair Display Bold (40px+)
- **Elegant Quotes**: Playfair Display Regular (24px)
- **Special Occasions**: Playfair Display Italic (20px)

## 📱 Platform-Specific Specifications

### Instagram

#### Feed Posts
- **Dimensions**: 1080x1080px (Square)
- **Alternative**: 1080x1350px (Portrait 4:5)
- **File Format**: JPG or PNG
- **Resolution**: 72 DPI minimum
- **File Size**: Under 30MB

#### Stories
- **Dimensions**: 1080x1920px (9:16 ratio)
- **Safe Zone**: 1080x1420px (center area)
- **File Format**: JPG, PNG, or MP4
- **Video Length**: 15 seconds maximum

#### Reels
- **Dimensions**: 1080x1920px (9:16 ratio)
- **Video Length**: 15-90 seconds
- **File Format**: MP4
- **Frame Rate**: 30 FPS minimum

### Facebook

- **File Format**: JPG or PNG

- **File Format**: JPG or PNG

## 🎨 Design Templates & Layouts

### Template 1: Product Showcase
```
Layout Structure:
- Background: GIFTPAL Purple gradient
- Product Image: Center, 60% of space
- Logo: Top right corner (small)
- Text Overlay: Bottom 25%
- CTA Button: Bottom right

Typography:
- Product Name: Inter Bold, 28px, White
- Description: Inter Regular, 16px, Light Gray
- Price: Inter Semibold, 20px, GIFTPAL Gold
```

### Template 2: Seller Spotlight
```
Layout Structure:
- Background: White with subtle pattern
- Seller Photo: Left 40% (circular crop)
- Content Area: Right 60%
- Brand Elements: GIFTPAL colors as accents

Typography:
- Seller Name: Playfair Display Bold, 32px, GIFTPAL Purple
- Business Name: Inter Semibold, 20px, Deep Black
- Description: Inter Regular, 16px, Neutral Gray
- Stats: Inter Bold, 18px, GIFTPAL Green
```

### Template 3: Gift Guide
```
Layout Structure:
- Header: GIFTPAL Purple background
- Grid Layout: 2x2 or 3x3 product grid
- Each Cell: Product image + price
- Footer: Call-to-action area

Typography:
- Title: Inter Bold, 36px, White
- Category: Inter Semibold, 24px, GIFTPAL Gold
- Product Names: Inter Medium, 14px, Deep Black
- Prices: Inter Bold, 16px, GIFTPAL Green
```

### Template 4: Educational Content
```
Layout Structure:
- Background: Cream with subtle texture
- Icon/Illustration: Top center
- Content Blocks: Numbered or bulleted
- GIFTPAL branding: Bottom corner

Typography:
- Headline: Playfair Display Bold, 40px, GIFTPAL Purple
- Tips/Points: Inter Regular, 18px, Deep Black
- Numbers: Inter Bold, 24px, GIFTPAL Gold
- Caption: Inter Medium, 14px, Neutral Gray
```

### Template 5: Quote/Testimonial
```
Layout Structure:
- Background: GIFTPAL Green gradient
- Quote Marks: Large, decorative
- Customer Photo: Small, circular
- Text: Center-aligned

Typography:
- Quote: Playfair Display Italic, 28px, White
- Customer Name: Inter Semibold, 18px, White
- Location: Inter Regular, 14px, Light Gray
```

## 📸 Photography Guidelines

### Product Photography
- **Lighting**: Natural, soft lighting preferred
- **Background**: Clean, uncluttered (white or branded colors)
- **Angles**: Multiple angles showing details
- **Context**: Lifestyle shots showing products in use
- **Quality**: High resolution, sharp focus

### Lifestyle Photography
- **Models**: Diverse Nigerian representation
- **Settings**: Authentic Nigerian environments
- **Emotions**: Joy, celebration, connection
- **Colors**: Complement brand palette
- **Authenticity**: Real moments, not overly staged

### Seller Photography
- **Environment**: Clean, professional workspace
- **Lighting**: Well-lit, avoiding harsh shadows
- **Composition**: Rule of thirds, engaging angles
- **Storytelling**: Show creation process
- **Branding**: Subtle GIFTPAL elements

## 🎬 Video Content Specifications

### Instagram Reels/Stories
- **Aspect Ratio**: 9:16 (vertical)
- **Resolution**: 1080x1920px minimum
- **Frame Rate**: 30 FPS
- **Length**: 15-60 seconds
- **Format**: MP4, H.264 codec

### Facebook Videos
- **Aspect Ratio**: 16:9 (landscape) or 1:1 (square)
- **Resolution**: 1280x720px minimum
- **Frame Rate**: 30 FPS
- **Length**: 15 seconds to 2 minutes
- **Format**: MP4, MOV

### YouTube Videos
- **Aspect Ratio**: 16:9 (landscape)
- **Resolution**: 1920x1080px (1080p)
- **Frame Rate**: 30 or 60 FPS
- **Length**: 2-10 minutes optimal
- **Format**: MP4, H.264 codec

## 🖼️ Graphic Design Elements

### Logo Usage
- **Minimum Size**: 24px height (digital), 15mm (print)
- **Clear Space**: 1x logo height on all sides
- **Backgrounds**: Use on white, light gray, or brand colors
- **Variations**: Full color, white, black, monogram

### Icons & Illustrations
- **Style**: Minimalist, line-based
- **Colors**: Brand palette only
- **Stroke Width**: 2px consistent
- **Corner Radius**: 4px for rounded elements
- **Size**: 24px, 32px, 48px, 64px standard sizes

### Patterns & Textures
- **Gift Pattern**: Subtle gift box outlines
- **Nigerian Motifs**: Traditional patterns as backgrounds
- **Geometric**: Simple shapes in brand colors
- **Opacity**: 10-20% for background elements

## 📊 Infographic Guidelines

### Data Visualization
- **Charts**: Use brand colors consistently
- **Fonts**: Inter family only
- **Hierarchy**: Size and color for importance
- **Spacing**: Generous white space
- **Icons**: Consistent style throughout

### Layout Principles
- **Grid System**: 12-column grid
- **Alignment**: Left-aligned text, centered graphics
- **Flow**: Top to bottom, left to right
- **Balance**: Equal visual weight distribution

## 📱 Mobile-First Design Principles

### Responsive Design
- **Mobile Priority**: Design for mobile first
- **Touch Targets**: Minimum 44px tap areas
- **Readability**: 16px minimum font size
- **Loading**: Optimize for slow connections
- **Accessibility**: High contrast, alt text

### User Experience
- **Simplicity**: Clean, uncluttered layouts
- **Navigation**: Intuitive, thumb-friendly
- **Speed**: Fast loading images and videos

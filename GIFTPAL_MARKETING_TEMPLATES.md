# 🎁 GIFTPAL Marketing Materials & Brand Guidelines

## 🎨 Brand Identity Guidelines

### Brand Positioning
**"Nigeria's Premier AI-Powered Gift Marketplace"**

**Mission Statement:**
"To connect thoughtful gift-givers with unique sellers while transforming Nigeria's gift economy through technology and personalization."

**Vision Statement:**
"To become Africa's leading gift marketplace platform, making meaningful gift-giving accessible to everyone."

**Brand Values:**
- **Thoughtfulness**: Every gift tells a story
- **Authenticity**: Genuine products from verified sellers
- **Innovation**: AI-powered personalization and discovery
- **Community**: Building connections between givers and creators
- **Excellence**: Premium experience at every touchpoint

### Visual Identity

#### Logo Usage
- **Primary Logo**: Full color GIFTPAL wordmark with gift box icon
- **Secondary Logo**: Simplified "GP" monogram for small applications
- **Minimum Size**: 24px height for digital, 15mm for print
- **Clear Space**: Minimum 1x logo height on all sides

#### Color Palette
- **Primary Purple**: #6B46C1 (Brand authority and premium positioning)
- **Secondary Gold**: #F59E0B (Celebration and joy)
- **Accent Green**: #10B981 (Success and growth)
- **Neutral Gray**: #6B7280 (Text and supporting elements)
- **Background White**: #FFFFFF (Clean and modern)

#### Typography
- **Primary Font**: Inter (Modern, clean, highly readable)
- **Secondary Font**: Playfair Display (Elegant, for headlines)
- **Body Text**: 16px Inter Regular
- **Headlines**: 32px+ Playfair Display Bold
- **Subheadings**: 24px Inter Semibold

#### Imagery Style
- **Photography**: Bright, natural lighting with warm tones
- **Composition**: Clean, uncluttered backgrounds
- **People**: Diverse, authentic moments of joy and connection
- **Products**: High-quality, detailed shots with lifestyle context
- **Illustrations**: Minimalist line art in brand colors

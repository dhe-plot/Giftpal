## 📋 AGREEMENT TERMS

### 1. PURPOSE

This Non-Disclosure Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into to facilitate discussions regarding a potential investment opportunity in GIFTPAL, Nigeria's AI-powered gift marketplace platform ("Transaction"). Both parties wish to exchange confidential information for the purpose of evaluating the Transaction.

### 2. DEFINITION OF CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION

**"Confidential Information"** means any and all non-public, proprietary, or confidential information disclosed by either party, including but not limited to:

#### For GIFTPAL:
- Business plans, strategies, and financial projections
- AI algorithms, software code, and technical specifications
- Customer data, seller information, and user analytics
- Market research, competitive analysis, and business intelligence
- Financial statements, management accounts, and operational metrics
- Partnership agreements, supplier relationships, and contracts
- Intellectual property, trade secrets, and proprietary methodologies
- Team information, organizational structure, and compensation details
- Product roadmaps, development plans, and strategic initiatives

#### For Investor:
- Investment strategies, portfolio information, and fund details
- Due diligence processes, evaluation criteria, and internal assessments
- Other portfolio companies' information and investment terms
- Fund performance data and investor information

### 3. OBLIGATIONS

Each party agrees to:

#### 3.1 Confidentiality
- **Maintain strict confidentiality** of all Confidential Information received
- **Not disclose** Confidential Information to any third party without prior written consent
- **Use Confidential Information solely** for evaluating the potential Transaction
- **Protect Confidential Information** with the same degree of care used for own confidential information, but not less than reasonable care

#### 3.2 Limited Disclosure
Confidential Information may only be disclosed to:
- **Directors, officers, employees, and advisors** who have a legitimate need to know
- **Professional advisors** (lawyers, accountants, consultants) bound by professional confidentiality
- **Potential co-investors** who have signed similar confidentiality agreements

#### 3.3 Notice Requirement
Before disclosing to permitted recipients, the receiving party must:
- **Inform recipients** of the confidential nature of the information
- **Ensure recipients** are bound by confidentiality obligations
- **Remain liable** for any breach by such recipients

### 4. EXCEPTIONS

This Agreement does not apply to information that:
- **Is publicly available** at the time of disclosure or becomes publicly available through no breach of this Agreement
- **Was known** to the receiving party prior to disclosure, as evidenced by written records
- **Is independently developed** by the receiving party without use of Confidential Information
- **Is received from a third party** who has the right to disclose such information
- **Must be disclosed** pursuant to law, regulation, or court order (with prior notice to disclosing party)

### 5. NO OBLIGATION TO PROCEED

This Agreement does not:
- **Create any obligation** to proceed with the Transaction
- **Grant any rights** in intellectual property or business opportunities
- **Constitute an offer** or commitment to invest or accept investment
- **Create any partnership** or joint venture relationship

### 6. RETURN OF INFORMATION

Upon written request or termination of discussions:
- **Return or destroy** all Confidential Information and copies
- **Provide written certification** of compliance within 30 days
- **Delete electronic copies** from all systems and devices
- **Confidentiality obligations continue** despite return of materials

### 7. REMEDIES

#### 7.1 Irreparable Harm
Both parties acknowledge that breach of this Agreement would cause **irreparable harm** for which monetary damages would be inadequate.

#### 7.2 Equitable Relief
The non-breaching party shall be entitled to:
- **Injunctive relief** and specific performance
- **Monetary damages** including consequential damages
- **Attorney's fees and costs** incurred in enforcement
- **Any other relief** deemed proper by a court of competent jurisdiction

### 8. TERM AND TERMINATION

#### 8.1 Duration
This Agreement shall remain in effect for **three (3) years** from the date of execution, unless terminated earlier by mutual written consent.

#### 8.2 Survival
Confidentiality obligations shall **survive termination** and continue for **five (5) years** after the end of the Agreement term.

### 9. GENERAL PROVISIONS

#### 9.1 Governing Law
This Agreement shall be governed by the **laws of the Federal Republic of Nigeria**.

#### 9.2 Jurisdiction
Any disputes shall be subject to the **exclusive jurisdiction of Nigerian courts**, with venue in Lagos State.

#### 9.3 Entire Agreement
This Agreement constitutes the **entire agreement** between the parties regarding confidentiality and supersedes all prior agreements.

#### 9.4 Amendment
This Agreement may only be **amended in writing** signed by both parties.

#### 9.5 Severability
If any provision is deemed invalid, the **remainder shall remain in full force** and effect.

#### 9.6 Counterparts
This Agreement may be executed in **counterparts and electronic signatures** shall be deemed valid.

## 💡 USAGE INSTRUCTIONS

### Before Sending:
1. **Customize company details** (RC number, address)
2. **Insert investor information** (name, firm, address)
3. **Review terms** with legal counsel if needed
4. **Save signed copies** in secure location

### Best Practices:
- **Always send NDA first** before detailed discussions
- **Don't share sensitive information** until NDA is signed
- **Keep detailed records** of what information is shared when
- **Follow up promptly** on unsigned NDAs
- **Store signed NDAs securely** for future reference

### Red Flags:
- **Investor refuses to sign** standard NDA terms
- **Requests unusual modifications** to confidentiality terms
- **Delays signing** without reasonable explanation
- **Asks for sensitive information** before NDA execution

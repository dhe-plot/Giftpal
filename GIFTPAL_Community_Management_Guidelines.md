# 🤝 GIFTPAL Community Management Guidelines

## 🎯 Community Management Philosophy

### Core Principles
- **Authenticity**: Genuine, human interactions over automated responses
- **Responsiveness**: Quick, helpful responses to all inquiries
- **Empathy**: Understanding and addressing customer emotions
- **Proactivity**: Anticipating needs and preventing issues
- **Cultural Sensitivity**: Respecting Nigerian values and traditions

### Brand Voice & Tone
- **Friendly**: Warm, approachable, and welcoming
- **Professional**: Knowledgeable and reliable
- **Helpful**: Solution-oriented and supportive
- **Celebratory**: Enthusiastic about gifts and special moments
- **Local**: Understanding of Nigerian culture and context

## ⏰ Response Time Standards

### Platform-Specific Response Times

#### WhatsApp Business
- **Immediate Response**: Auto-reply within 1 minute
- **Human Response**: Within 15 minutes during business hours
- **Complex Issues**: Within 1 hour
- **Business Hours**: 8 AM - 8 PM (Monday-Saturday), 10 AM - 6 PM (Sunday)

#### Instagram Direct Messages
- **Response Time**: Within 30 minutes during business hours
- **After Hours**: Within 2 hours or next business day
- **Stories Mentions**: Within 1 hour

#### Facebook Messenger
- **Response Time**: Within 30 minutes during business hours
- **Automated Greeting**: Immediate
- **Complex Issues**: Within 1 hour

#### Twitter Mentions/DMs
- **Public Mentions**: Within 15 minutes
- **Direct Messages**: Within 30 minutes
- **Crisis Management**: Within 5 minutes

#### LinkedIn Messages
- **B2B Inquiries**: Within 2 hours during business hours
- **General Messages**: Within 4 hours

#### Email Support
- **General Inquiries**: Within 2 hours
- **Technical Issues**: Within 1 hour
- **Complaints**: Within 30 minutes

## 📱 Platform-Specific Guidelines

### Instagram Community Management

#### Daily Tasks
- Monitor and respond to comments on posts
- Reply to direct messages
- Check story mentions and tags
- Engage with user-generated content
- Share relevant stories to main story
- Monitor hashtags (#GIFTPAL, #GIFTPALStory)

#### Engagement Strategy
- Like and respond to all genuine comments
- Ask follow-up questions to encourage conversation
- Share user posts to stories (with permission)
- Create polls and interactive stories

#### Content Moderation
- Remove spam and inappropriate comments

- Address legitimate complaints publicly and professionally

#### Community Building

## 🚨 Crisis Management Protocol

### Crisis Identification
- **Level 1**: Individual customer complaints
- **Level 2**: Multiple similar complaints or negative viral content
- **Level 3**: Major platform issues or widespread negative sentiment
- **Level 4**: Legal issues, safety concerns, or major PR crisis

### Response Protocol
1. **Immediate Assessment**: Determine crisis level within 15 minutes
2. **Internal Notification**: Alert management team immediately
3. **Response Strategy**: Develop response within 30 minutes
4. **Public Response**: Issue statement within 1 hour
5. **Follow-up**: Regular updates until resolution

### Crisis Communication Templates
```
"We're aware of the issue and are investigating immediately. We'll provide an update within the next hour. Thank you for your patience."

"We've identified the issue and are working on a solution. Affected customers will be contacted directly. We sincerely apologize for any inconvenience."

"Issue resolved. We've implemented additional measures to prevent this from happening again. Thank you for your patience and continued trust."
```

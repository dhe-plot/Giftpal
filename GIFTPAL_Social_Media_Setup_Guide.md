## 🚀 Launch Strategy

### Pre-Launch (2 weeks before platform launch):
1. Set up all social media accounts
2. Create and schedule 2 weeks of content
3. Begin following relevant accounts and engaging
4. Start building anticipation with "Coming Soon" posts

### Launch Week:
1. Announce platform launch across all channels
2. Share launch event highlights
3. Feature founding sellers and their stories
4. Encourage user-generated content with hashtags

### Post-Launch (Ongoing):
1. Regular content posting (daily on Instagram/Facebook, 3x/week on LinkedIn)
2. Community engagement and customer service
3. Influencer partnerships and collaborations
4. Paid advertising campaigns for growth
